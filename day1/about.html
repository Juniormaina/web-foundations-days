About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple application that helps you capture and organize your ideas in one place. It allows you to quickly write down reminders and important thoughts before you forget them.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page to view your notes.
  2. Type your idea into the New note input field.
  3. Click the Add Note button to add your note.
  4. Review your notes in the Your notes section.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas and reminders.
  • View your notes in an organized list.
  • Simple and easy-to-use interface.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for common actions.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note.
Ctrl + S Save your current note.
